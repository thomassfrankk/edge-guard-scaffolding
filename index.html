About Us

Local Height Safety Specialists

Edge Guard & Scaffolding (EGS) was built on a simple idea: every tradesperson deserves to come home safe. We supply and install certified edge protection and scaffolding for builders, roofers and trade contractors across Brisbane — from Ipswich and Logan to the Redlands, Moreton Bay and the Gold Coast hinterland.

Every job is handled by licensed, experienced crews who know Australian WHS requirements inside out. Whether it's a single-storey renovation or a multi-level commercial build, we get your site protected fast so your trades can get to work without delay.

We work directly with builders, roofers and site supervisors to schedule around your build program — because scaffolding and edge protection should never be the thing holding up your job.
